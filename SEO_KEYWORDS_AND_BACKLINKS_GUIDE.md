## 4. Anchor Text Strategy & Guidelines

When acquiring backlinks, the clickable text (the "anchor text") must be carefully balanced so Google recognizes your authority without triggering spam filters.

```
Anchor Text Target Distribution:
├── 50% Brand Anchors       ["ADJP Creative", "adjpcreative", "ADJP", "adjpcreative.com"]
├── 25% Founder Anchors     ["Sruthiy Prabha", "Music Director Sruthiy Prabha", "Director Sruthiy Prabha"]
├── 15% Hybrid Anchors      ["ADJP Creative video production", "commercials by ADJP Creative", "films by Sruthiy Prabha"]
└── 10% Natural / URL       ["https://adjpcreative.com", "visit website", "source", "our website"]
```

> [!CAUTION]
> Avoid spamming 100% exact-match keywords like `"best advertising agency colombo"` as the sole anchor text. A healthy brand profile is primarily branded (`ADJP Creative`) and person-centric (`Sruthiy Prabha`).
